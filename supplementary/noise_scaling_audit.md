# Supplementary: what unitary folding amplifies

Generated by `experiments/v2/noise_scaling_audit.py`. Exact density-matrix values; no sampling.

## 1. Gate noise vs readout noise under folding

| noise present | <ZZ> at scale 1 | scale 3 | scale 5 | behaviour |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| gate noise only (depolarising 1e-2) | 0.990000 | 0.970299 | 0.950990 | amplified |
| readout noise only (p=0.05) | 1.000000 | 1.000000 | 1.000000 | **exactly invariant** |

Gate folding therefore cannot mitigate readout error. This confirms the point made by
Majumdar *et al.* (IEEE QCE 2023, Sec. V); it is stated here as a verification, not a new finding.

## 2. Realised gate counts per scale factor

Base circuit (`prism`, p=1): `sx`=18, `x`=0, `rz`=39, `cx`=18

| nominal scale | cx | sx | x | rz | cx ratio | sx ratio | uniform over error-carrying gates? |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2 | 30 | 42 | 0 | 127 | 1.67 | 2.33 | **no** |
| 3 | 54 | 54 | 0 | 189 | 3.00 | 3.00 | yes |
| 4 | 66 | 78 | 0 | 277 | 3.67 | 4.33 | **no** |
| 5 | 90 | 90 | 0 | 339 | 5.00 | 5.00 | yes |

At **odd** scales the error-carrying gates fold a whole number of times. `rz` does not,
but `rz` is error-free in every noise model used here:

- `dev_lagos`: `noise_instructions = ['cx', 'id', 'measure', 'reset', 'sx', 'x']` (no `rz`)
- `dev_algiers`: `noise_instructions = ['cx', 'id', 'measure', 'reset', 'sx', 'x']` (no `rz`)

**Caveat.** Equal gate counts are necessary but *not sufficient* for uniform effective noise
scaling: coherent errors, crosstalk, and idle/thermal evolution during the longer folded circuit
are not captured by a count ratio. We therefore validate ideal equivalence directly
(`tests/test_v2_correctness.py`, tests T1/T2/T5) and do not claim odd factors prove uniform scaling.
